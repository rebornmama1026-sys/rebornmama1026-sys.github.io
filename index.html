About Us

More Than Confinement Care — A New Beginning for Every Mother

Reborn Mama 位于马来西亚柔佛新山，是一家精品月子中心。我们以「低密度 · 科学照护 · 专业产康」为理念，打造温馨、私密、舒适的月子环境。

Reborn Mama is a boutique confinement centre in Johor Bahru, Malaysia. We provide a warm, peaceful and personalised environment guided by our core philosophy.
